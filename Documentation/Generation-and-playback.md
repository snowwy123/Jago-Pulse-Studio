# Generation and playback

Choose **Customise generation** beside Generate (or above the canvas). Settings apply to the next composition; the current scenes stay intact until you generate.

- **Visuals:** choose a recipe, range or checked families/looks, layering and whether to include text.
- **Colour:** keep your palette, choose the next palette, and choose which colour roles stay fixed or move. These controls target the next generation while this dialog is open.
- **Movement:** set the additional drift and form movement. Each family retains its own native animation. The Visual workshop opens deeper editing for the current scene.
- **Timing & transitions:** select pacing and beat grouping, then use suggested transitions, one style, or a checked mixture. Transition length is capped for short scenes. Locked scenes remain protected.

Use **Generate with these choices** to build the result. Save promising versions in My directions before exploring further.

## Music and navigation

When importing music, match the composition to the track or choose an excerpt. Generation respects that selected duration. The playback bar covers the full composition, displays minutes and seconds, and resumes playback after scrubbing if it was playing. A section jump selector appears beside the time counter when song sections are present.

Song sections and detected beats are estimates unless you have checked them. Open the music timing/sections controls to correct the grid, name sections and move boundaries. Section labels alone do not guarantee accurate verse/chorus recognition.

Both visuals remain animated through a transition. Authored entrances/exits finish at their intended layer endpoints while procedural motion continues. Backward seeking rebuilds feedback consistently.

## Preview quality

Choose Economy, Light, Balanced or Detailed preview quality for your machine. Economy reduces preview size, complexity and update rate; it does not reduce saved project settings or export resolution. Particularly complex visuals can still be expensive. Exports use their own size and quality settings.
