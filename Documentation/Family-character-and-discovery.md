# Family animation and discovery

Visual styling and intrinsic behaviour are separate. Directional drift is an optional additional movement, and turning it off should leave the family recognisable.

## Restored behaviours

- Curl streams integrates curved paths through a moving vector field; turbulence changes the swirls.
- Waveform ribbons uses the preceding three seconds of analysed energy and bass envelopes, rather than a decorative sine wave with a single loudness multiplier. This is an envelope visualisation, not a sample-accurate oscilloscope.
- Oscilloscope forms uses frequency-band values to shape its Lissajous traces.
- Ribbon helix uses paired strands; every cross-link is computed from those same strands.
- Chromatic blobs drift their individual centres while their contours deform.
- Concentric fans rotates partial arcs instead of substituting expanding ripple loops.
- Cascading words falls through clipped rows; its stepped and ribbon variations retain different movement.
- Editorial motion and Type stack retain readable internal movement. The classic Kinetic type entrance still uses its intended layer slide/rise motion.
- Orbital lattice respects its editable symmetry value.

The earlier restored spirals, kaleidoscope reflections, branch growth and ink expansion remain. Generated family-response defaults do not add whole-field drift to the restored graphic families. Existing authored drift, locks, keyframes, palettes and timing remain editable. Audio-derived geometry and optional extra music modulation are separate: disabling extra reaction leaves the visual's source data available; music bypass suppresses source-driven shaping as well.

## Original flow

Its existing fourteen looks now have four discovery groups: Currents & silk, Ink & organic, Mirrors & spirals, and Geometry & signals. Browse has a group selector; Choose families & looks has group-only generation actions. These groups organise the existing engine without changing saved look IDs.

## Random selection

In Choose families & looks, Equal chance per look is the default. It shuffles checked family/look pairs and cycles through them before repeating, so a large family does not hide its individual looks. Equal chance per family remains available: it chooses a family, then a look, avoiding consecutive repeats of a family where possible. This setting controls checked selection; broad unfiltered composition recipes retain their own art-direction rules. All families & looks provides a quick route to the complete checked pool. Close and variations modes deliberately keep one direction per composition; weighting affects that direction's initial choice.

Generation preserves locked work. A short film can end before every checked look has appeared.
