# Sound to Pulse: authored music handoff

Sound 0.13.0 / Pulse 0.18.0 development preview. Everything runs offline.

In Sound, choose **Send to → Pulse**. **Include authored tempo, sections and drum cues for Pulse** is enabled for complete arrangements. Pulse reviews the import before changing your film. Choose whether to match the film length, use Sound's loop/ending and add gentle starter reactions. Existing manual sections, corrected cues and mappings are protected by default. Visual layers remain intact. Cancel leaves the project unchanged; importing is one undoable change.

This transfers Sound's actual tempo, section ranges and scheduled drum events, including their humanised timing. It does not infer chorus labels or isolate drum stems. Dense arrangements retain up to 2,048 cues and report truncation. Single sound effects use audio-only delivery. Ordinary WAV/MP3 import still uses Pulse's estimated analysis.

If the apps are separate, save a Sound transfer and open it in Pulse through **Timing → Open Sound audio transfer…**, Add artwork/music, or drag and drop. The optional editable Sound original can be saved from the review. Sending a revision is a fresh reviewed import, not a continuously linked project.

Try **Example projects → Sound + Pulse / Afterglow drive** or **Sound + Pulse / Window seat** in Pulse. Both contain playable original Sound music and exact authored timing. Full-quality WAVs, compact playback and editable Sound/Pulse projects are in Examples/Music demos. These are original bundled demonstrations, not user tracks.

Checks cover cancel, undo, audio-only mode, protected manual edits, invalid metadata fallback, embedded-audio validation, actual native new/existing-window delivery and the native receive command used by Workscape. They do not establish subjective musical quality or accuracy of inferred song structure.
