# Original Pulse visual studies

Voltage garden and Living contours are original Pulse implementations. They are visual studies inspired by Martin's **acid wiring** and Geiss's **Reaction Diffusion 2**, encountered through the credited Butterchurn preset collection. They are not ports of those presets and do not require Butterchurn.

References:
- Butterchurn and its credited preset collection: https://github.com/jberg/butterchurn and https://github.com/jberg/butterchurn-presets
- MilkDrop preset-authoring concepts (warp, history, sources and compositing): https://www.geisswerks.com/milkdrop/milkdrop_preset_authoring.html
- Gray-Scott reaction-diffusion explanation by Karl Sims: https://www.karlsims.com/rd.html

Living contours implements two chemical concentrations with a local diffusion stencil, feed/loss controls, authored seeding and musical advection. Voltage garden uses Pulse-authored circuit traces, moving highlights and feedback. No artist preset equations, shaders or third-party renderer code were copied into these implementations. The separate earlier Butterchurn comparison notices remain with that experiment's source; they do not describe the capabilities of the original flow renderer.
