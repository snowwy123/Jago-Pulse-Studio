# Sound + Pulse music examples

Choose Sound + Pulse / Afterglow drive or Window seat in Pulse Example projects. Compact offline music is embedded in the Pulse files; original full-quality WAVs are included separately. Afterglow drive is a stronger house-informed loop; Window seat is a softer laid-back composition. Both use original Sound synthesis, not external recordings or private user music.

Open .jagomusic in Sound to edit notes, instruments and arrangement. WAV is the rendered 44.1kHz stereo music; .jagopulse is the authored visual composition. Its section labels and drum cues come from the actual Sound arrangement/events, including humanisation, not guessed chorus or drum recognition.

Changing the Sound project and re-exporting does not automatically update Pulse cue/section timing. Reimport the music and revise the authored controls as needed.
